Summary Formula

  • Present = total present credit from day status. P = 1, H/P or P/H = 0.5, P/CL or CL/P = 0.5, CCL = 1 (CCL applied on holiday overrides H).
  • Working Days = total period days - holiday credit. Sunday = 1, role holiday H/F = 1, H/H = 0.5.
  • Worked = CL + CCL + WL + OD + VL + ML + Present + Holiday. AB and LOP are NOT subtracted (already excluded from Present). Minimum shown is 0.
  • Permission = count of permission days in the period. Full day = 1, half day = 0.5. Includes PER/P, P/PER, and approved PER leave entries.
  • WL = total WL (Work from Home) leave days used in the selected period.
  • Holiday = total holiday credit counted for the period.
