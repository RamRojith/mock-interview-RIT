Approvers Assignment Help
  1. Select a creator role from the creator role dropdown.
  2. Drag the required roles from Available Roles to Approval Hierarchy.
  3. Reorder the roles inside the hierarchy by dragging them up or down.
  4. When an approver belongs to another department, select Cross Dept and choose the appropriate department.
  5. Click Save Approvers to store the hierarchy.
